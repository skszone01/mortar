# Product

## Register

product

## Users
ARMA REFORGER players calculating mortar ballistic solutions during active gameplay. They need fast, highly-legible data access on a secondary monitor, half-screen split, or a mobile device.

## Product Purpose
Provide precise, rapid elevation, azimuth, charge, and time of flight calculations for ARMA REFORGER mortars. Ensure accuracy and ease of use under pressure.

## Brand Personality
Tactical, Rugged, High-Tech HUD, Precise. 3-word personality: Tactical, Precise, Functional.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS dashboards (pastel colors, cards within cards, rounded playful buttons, soft illustration). Cluttered and slow page-load animations.

## Design Principles
1. **Mission-Critical Legibility**: Visual hierarchy prioritized for fast reading in dark environments. Monospace for numerical calculations and tactical readouts.
2. **Device Flexibility**: Seamless layout adaptation for full-screen, half-screen, and mobile layouts.
3. **Immersive Utility**: Design that feels like a functional military HUD/instrument panel, not a standard website, while maintaining strict usability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
High-contrast dark mode for low-light gaming environments (WCAG AA compliant contrast for text/inputs). No distracting autoplay animations.
